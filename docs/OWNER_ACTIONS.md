# Owner actions

These are the only actions that require the owner’s accounts, judgment, or private records:

1. **Credentials:** enter owner-controlled secrets in the deployment machine’s untracked `.env` file. Keep payment destinations empty until verified and keep any administrator key out of browsers, source control, logs, and screenshots.
2. **Account verification:** verify any bank, model-provider, or distribution account directly with that provider before enabling its integration. The app does not infer that a plan is free or that a credential is valid.
3. **Client approval:** review and approve the scope, quote, and any first outreach or public change. Drafts remain queued until an owner decision.
4. **Bank reconciliation:** compare each buyer claim against the receiving-account statement and attest only to funds actually received. A screenshot, QR, or buyer reference is not payment proof.
